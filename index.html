FAQ

Questions, answered

Who can apply?

University students (undergrad or grad) and recent graduates, 18 or older, from any major.

I'm new to hackathons. Can I join?

Yes. We run beginner workshops, mentors walk the floor, and we help solo hackers find a team.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. The hackathon is free for accepted hackers, with meals on both days.

Is it overnight?

No. Doors are open 9 AM–9 PM on Saturday and 9 AM–6 PM on Sunday. You can keep building at home.

How do teams work?

Teams have 2–4 people. Everyone applies on their own. No team yet? Join team matching at the opening.

What should I bring?

Your laptop, charger and a photo ID. We'll have a hardware lab with basic tools and parts.

Can I start building before the event?

Plan and learn in advance, but build during the hackathon. Open-source code, public APIs and AI tools are fine. Just credit them.

Where is it?

In the Los Angeles area. We'll post the address, parking and transit options as soon as the venue is confirmed.

Code of Conduct

Su&IT AI Hackathon is for everyone. We are committed to a friendly, safe and harassment-free event for all participants, mentors, judges, partners, volunteers and staff, regardless of gender, gender identity, age, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion or tech stack.

  • Be kind and respectful. Critique ideas, not people.
  • No harassment, intimidation, discrimination or unwanted attention, in person or online.
  • Keep it safe: follow venue rules, hardware-lab safety briefings and organizer instructions.
  • Build honestly: no plagiarism. Credit the open-source code, APIs and AI tools you use.
  • Respect privacy: don't photograph, record or share others without their consent.

If something happens, talk to any organizer on site or email info@suit.by. We will act quickly and confidentially. Organizers may warn, disqualify or remove anyone who breaks these rules.

Privacy & cookies

We collect only what you submit in these forms (such as your name, email, school and answers) to review applications, contact you about the event and plan logistics.

  • Your data is stored in Su&IT's Google Workspace and is accessible only to the organizing team.
  • We never sell your data. We share hacker applications with hiring partners only if you tick the opt-in box.
  • We delete application data within 12 months after the event, or sooner on request: email info@suit.by.
  • Photos and video may be taken at the event. Tell the registration desk if you'd rather not be photographed.
  • Cookies: this page remembers your cookie choice in your browser. Visits are counted only if you accept.
